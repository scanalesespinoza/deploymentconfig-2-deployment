# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In this project, we pledge to create an inclusive, welcoming, and respectful environment for everyone. We value diversity and believe that all contributors should feel safe and supported. We do not tolerate harassment, discriminatory behavior, or any form of intimidation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:
- Issues, Pull Requests, code reviews, and discussions on GitHub.
- Official communication channels (Slack, Discord, etc.) and any other project-related media.
- In-person or virtual events organized by the project team.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat everyone with respect and courtesy.
- Be mindful of how your words and actions may be perceived by others with different backgrounds.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully and respond professionally.
- Show empathy and support, especially to newcomers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Discriminatory comments or actions based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, religion, age, or any other protected characteristic.
- Use of profanity, insults, slurs, or demeaning language.
- Threats, harassment, or hate speech.
- Public or private harassment, or threatening behavior.
- Sharing explicit or offensive content.
- Any behavior that undermines the safe and collaborative environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:
- Moderating inappropriate comments, commits, or other content that violates this Code of Conduct.
- Issuing warnings or requesting changes from individuals violating the guidelines.
- Removing privileges or expelling individuals from the project for repeated or severe violations.
- Ensuring a safe and respectful environment for all participants.

## Consequences of Unacceptable Behavior

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by anyone. The project maintainers will review and determine appropriate action, which may include:
1. Private warning or request for revision.
2. Removal of offending content.
3. Temporary or permanent ban from the project repository or communication channels.
4. Reporting to platform administrators or other authorities if necessary.
